## Can the first player take half?

Alice and Bob take turns removing a vertex and collecting its weight. Every move must leave the other vertices connected. Alice goes first. On a bipartite graph with an even number of vertices, can she always take home at least half the weight?

The proof gives her a strategy when adjacent positive weights agree and the graph excludes one particular branching shape: a three-armed claw with each edge subdivided once. Alice can take an available positive vertex when its weight covers Bob’s reply. When only zero-weight moves are available, she targets the heavier side of the bipartition. The released paper also compares two possible last moves on a path with attached leaves. The general game remains open.
