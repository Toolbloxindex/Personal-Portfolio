## Can every point belong to exactly one ribbon?

Imagine 7,776 points, each described by five coordinates between 0 and 5. Armstrong’s question divides them into slices according to the sum of their coordinates. Inside each slice, the challenge is to group the points into chains of six. Each step must move up in Armstrong’s order with no other point strictly between its endpoints.

For $a=6$, five slices supply 778 explicit chains. Reflecting the construction supplies another 518. Together the 1,296 ribbons cover every point exactly once across all nine slices. A separate checker verifies both the coverage and the steps. This settles the entire $a=6$ case; the conjecture for arbitrary $a$ remains open.
