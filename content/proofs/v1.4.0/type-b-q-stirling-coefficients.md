## Where does strictness begin?

Take three adjacent coefficients, square the middle one, and subtract the product of the other two. A positive gap means that the middle coefficient sits strictly above the geometric mean of its neighbors.

In column one of the second-kind type B q-Stirling array, rank five still has an equality. From rank six onward every interior gap is positive. The combined paper brings this threshold together with results about every other coefficient in the first-kind array and on the second diagonal of both families. Seven Lean declarations give the precise scope of the collection.
